# 🧟‍♂️ Operational Overview: Project Breakout

## 1. Core Premise
A top-secret biological experiment conducted by BioCorp at an underground research facility experiences a critical containment failure. An airborne mutating pathogen escapes into the surrounding civilian sector, turning the local populace into hyper-aggressive infected within hours. 

You play as **Sergeant Vance**, an elite military operative deployed on a high-stakes solo extraction mission into the quarantined residential zone to locate, protect, and evacuate trapped survivors before a tactical airstrike sanitizes the city.

---

## 2. Main Characters

* **Sergeant Vance (Protagonist):** A battle-hardened tactical specialist equipped with high-tech gear and heavy weaponry.
* **Dr. Elena Rostova (Key Survivor):** A lead researcher trapped in her home who holds the biological data needed to stop the pathogen.
* **Civilian Survivors:** Terrified residents hiding in attics, basements, and reinforced safe rooms across the district.

---

## 3. Narrative Campaign Structure

### Act I: The Deployment
* **Location:** Suburban Residential District
* **Situation:** Vance drops into the outer edge of the quarantine zone. The streets are overrun with basic Walker zombies.
* **Objective:** Clear barricades, breach 3 residential houses, and rescue stranded families.

### Act II: Deep Breach
* **Location:** High-Rise Apartment Complex
* **Situation:** The infection mutates rapidly. Fast Runners and acid-spitting mutations begin stalking Vance through tight hallways.
* **Objective:** Fight floor-by-floor up to Dr. Rostova's apartment and retrieve her data drive.

### Act III: Final Evacuation
* **Location:** City Square & Military Evac Point
* **Situation:** A massive Tank mutation (the original failed lab experiment) blocks access to the extraction helicopter.
* **Objective:** Protect Dr. Rostova and the rescued civilians while defeating the Tank boss before the airstrike arrives.
